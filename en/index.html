A Brazilian platform to help you practice more English

ETT brings together digital practice tools (the ETT Player), free conversation meetings and partners. It is not a course: there are no classes or levels to pass — it is support for your own practice.

What ETT is

Practice tools

The ETT Player gathers the digital tools we built for daily practice. Its interface is in Portuguese.

Conversation meetings

A free online meeting every Monday and an in-person one in Curitiba on Saturdays. Open to anyone.

Partners

Courses, immersions and mentoring are offered by partner companies, not by ETT. Their programs are in Portuguese.

Questions from outside Brazil

What language is the meeting in?
English. Most participants and the moderation team are Brazilian, so you will hear a range of accents and levels.
Do I need to speak Portuguese?
Not for the meeting. The tools (ETT Player) and the rest of the site are in Portuguese.
Can I pay from outside Brazil?
The meetings are always free. The tools in the ETT Player are open to try. The guided activation (one-on-one guidance, a practice plan and personalized material) costs R$ 97, charged in Brazilian reais through our Brazilian checkout — PIX or card, if your card accepts payments in BRL.
Is the rest of the site in English?
No — this page is the only one in English. The full site is in Portuguese.
